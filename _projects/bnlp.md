BNLP (Biotech Natural Language Processing) is an algorithm that analyzes daily biotech news to predict its potential impact on the stock prices of related companies. Building it gave valuable insight into the drug development process and sharpened natural language processing skills — an exciting blend of finance, biotechnology, and advanced computational techniques.
